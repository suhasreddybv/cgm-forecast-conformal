## Sensor-versus-reference caveat

The iCGM criteria govern agreement between a **sensor** and a **laboratory reference** (comparator) measurement taken at the same time. Nothing in this repository is a sensor measurement: the forecasts are compared with a CGM (D-007), at a horizon of 30 or 60 minutes. Any criterion applied here is therefore an **analogue** — the same tolerance and percentage, applied to forecast-versus-CGM at a horizon — and is labelled as such everywhere it appears. A forecast "meeting" an iCGM analogue is not a claim about device clearance.
